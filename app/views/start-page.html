Create and manage a call for sites form

Create a call for sites form and manage sites submitted for your local plan.

Applies to England

Contents

  1. Overview
  2. Who can use this service
  3. Sign into the service

Overview

Use this service to create and manage a call for sites form for your local planning authority.

Once you have signed in, you can:

  • review the standard questions included in the form
  • add one local constraints question if you need to
  • publish a link to share with site submitters
  • view and download site submissions
  • invite your team members to access site submissions

Who can use this service

You can use this service if you work for a local planning authority in England and are responsible for managing a call for sites process.

Sign into the service

You will need to sign into this service with your council email address.
