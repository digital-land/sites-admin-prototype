Review the questions in the call for sites form

Section one: submitting on behalf of someone else

Questions in this section:

  • Are you submitting this site on behalf of someone else?
  • What type of person or organisation are you submitting this site on behalf of?
  • What is their main connection to the site?
  • Enter their contact details

Section two: your details

Questions in this section:

  • Which best describes you or your organisation?
  • What is your main connection to the site?
  • Enter your contact details

Section three: site location and boundary

Site submitters can find the site location and boundary by:

  • entering a postcode
  • finding it on a map

Submitters will add a description to the site boundary.

Section four: constraints

We identify potential constraints by checking the site with the Planning Data Platform (link opens in new tab).

For each identified constraint, we will ask the site submitter:

  • Will you mitigate this constraint?
  • Tell us how you will mitigate this constraint

Other questions in this section:

  • Are you aware of any other constraints?
  • Tell us about the other constraints and how you will mitigate them

Section five: site use

Questions in this section:

  • What is the site currently used for?
  • What is the current status of the site?
  • Are there existing uses on this site with a lease?
  • Why is the current status of the site unknown?
  • Are there existing uses on this site with a lease?
  • When do the current leases expire?
  • When are tenants expected to move out of the site?
  • What could the site be used for?
  • How many homes will this site create?
  • How much non-residential floorspace are you proposing?
  • Does the landowner support the development of this land?
  • Are you willing for this site to be joined with adjacent sites or used for multiple developments?
  • Does the site have multiple land owners?
  • Enter the names and addresses of the site owners
  • Are there any agreements between owners about developing this site?
  • Are there any access issues with the site?
  • Is there existing vehicle access to and from the public highway?
  • Explain how you will provide vehicle access to and from the public highway

Section six: additional information

Questions in this section:

  • Does the site have any contamination or pollution constraints?
  • Tell us what dangerous substances are at this site
  • Does the site have any of these constraints? (optional)
  • Tell us about these constraints
  • Has the site been marketed?
  • Tell us how the site has been marketed
  • When do you expect the site to be ready for development? (optional)
  • When do you expect the development to be complete? (optional)
  • Add any other comments about the site (optional)
